Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-07 · TaxRefund Bot

1. Information the app stores

  • Website visits: a random visitor ID in the first-party trb_visitor cookie, plus the referrer and browser user-agent. The cookie is HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax, Secure on HTTPS, and currently expires after one year.
  • Demand events: visit, quiz-start, quiz-complete, checklist-interest, and Telegram-open event types, with referrer and user-agent. An event is associated with a visitor only when the cookie matches a visitor record already stored by the app. A client-supplied ID is not accepted as proof of identity.
  • AI beta limits: the app stores request timestamps and either a Telegram chat ID or website visitor ID in Cloudflare D1 to enforce usage limits. It does not store AI prompts or generated answers in D1.
  • Telegram bot: Telegram chat ID, quiz state, and selected quiz categories are stored in Cloudflare D1 to operate the quiz.
  • Email: new email collection is disabled. Earlier versions may have stored email addresses in D1. No production database was inspected or changed for this update; an operator must review any historical records using authorized production access.

2. Information not requested

The quiz uses broad category choices and does not need exact income or tax-document data. Do not send Social Security numbers, bank details, complete tax returns, W-2/1099 documents, or identity documents in a website field or Telegram message.

3. Processing by service providers

Cloudflare hosts the Worker and D1 database. Telegram processes bot messages and may retain chat messages and records under its own systems and policies. When AI answers are enabled, a website or Telegram user's question and the app's instructions are sent to Groq for processing. The app does not control or promise deletion of records held by Telegram, Groq, Cloudflare, or other providers.

4. Deletion and retention

Send /delete to the bot in a private chat to delete the app's saved quiz state and that chat's AI-usage counters from D1. Website AI-usage timestamps are removed when a later quota reservation prunes expired rows. The command does not delete Telegram chat history, Telegram records, provider-side records, website visitor records, or interest events. Website analytics records have no automatic retention job in the current app; no fixed deletion schedule is promised for AI-usage rows beyond quota-window pruning.

5. Cookies and attribution

The random visitor cookie is used to count visits, associate later demand events with an existing visitor record, and scope website AI usage limits. It is not a login, does not identify a person, and is not used for cross-site tracking by this app.

6. Purchases and checklist

The Evidence Checklist is not available. The displayed $5 amount is hypothetical test pricing. The interest button records demand only; no order or payment is taken and no document is generated or delivered.

7. Changes

This policy describes the current app behavior and may be updated when that behavior changes. It does not claim that provider-side practices or legal requirements have been independently verified.
